1. The stories

The game has two standalone cases, with the same detective, the same engine and the same home city. You can play them in any order, but case 2 takes place one day after case 1.

Case 1: The Silence of the Hotel Alvorada

Sector 4, 2085. A data storm has cut off the city's upper district. The flying cars are grounded and the acid rain won't stop. At the top of the city stands the Hotel Grand Alvorada, a decaying art deco skyscraper.

At midnight, in Suite 404, biotech tycoon Arthur Pendelton is found dead. The door was locked from the inside with a quantum lock. Four people had motives. None of them, it seems, had a way in.

You are Detective Gabriel Vance. Examine the scene, question the suspects, comb the city for clues, connect the evidence and, when you're sure, gather everyone in the lobby and name the culprit. But be careful: an accusation without proof, or against the wrong person, could cost you the case and your badge.

Case 2: The Echo of Dead Data

Neo-Sampa, 2085. One day after the Alvorada case, the phone rings again. On the 220th floor of the Neo-Sampa Tower, headquarters of Synapse Corp, neural security engineer Kaelen Voss lies dead on the lab floor, next to his own interface chair. His brain, intact. His consciousness, erased.

The lab is shielded and was sealed by quantum encryption: no signal got in or out. A locked room, both physical and digital. The trail takes Vance from the corporate tower to the viaducts of the Minhocão, the bars of Bixiga and clinics that don't appear on any map, always by Aerotrain.

Three acts

Each case is divided into three acts. Each act begins when Vance reaches a major conclusion, and each conclusion unlocks new places and new questions.

2. Getting started

  • The title screen shows the two cases, each on a card with a % complete bar, the current act, the number of pieces of evidence and the date you last played.
  • ▶ Open the case starts the case from scratch. ▶ Continue case picks up where you left off: progress is saved automatically.
  • Each case has its own save. Playing one never erases the other. Restart erases only that case's progress (the game asks for confirmation).
  • The percentage rises with evidence and deductions and tops out at 95%; the last 5% is reserved for closing the case with the perfect ending.
  • Watch intro replays the case's opening. Each case has its own. Scenes advance with a tap, click or key press.
  • Language lets you choose Portuguese, English, Spanish, German, French, Italian or Korean. The whole game switches language.
Start by examining everything at the crime scene (Suite 404 in case 1, the Synapse lab in case 2). Your current objective is always shown at the top of the screen, just below the name of the location.

4. The game screen

  • Top left: current act, location name and the current objective.
  • Hunger, Thirst and Fatigue bars: see section 7.
  • Clock: time passes as you play.
  • Top buttons: GPS (outdoors only), Scanner, Evidence, Clues and Menu.
  • Yellow ! over a person: they have something new to talk about.
  • Yellow diamond floating over an object: there is still evidence to collect there. Blue diamond: the evidence only appears with the scanner on.
  • Green arrow on the ground: a door or passage.
  • Holocalls: during a call, portraits of both sides of the line appear; whoever is speaking is highlighted.
  • Countdown (only at one point in case 2): it replaces the objective at the top of the screen.

5. How to investigate

Examine

Walk up to objects and press the action button. A lot of evidence hides in ordinary things: a piece of furniture, a terminal, an item of clothing.

Forensic scanner

Vance's implant reveals what the eye can't see: heat traces, chemical residue, implants, devices. Switch the scanner on and examine places and people again. Some evidence only shows up this way.

With the scanner on, Vance is more alert. Walking through dangerous places with it off can bring surprises.

Conversations

  • Each character has a list of topics. New topics appear as you discover things.
  • Topics marked ⚠ are confrontations: Vance uses a piece of evidence against the person. They usually lead to the best revelations.
  • Some conversations offer choices (for example, being kind or tough). Each approach can get different answers.
  • You can also present a piece of evidence to any character to see how they react.
  • People on the street, police officers and regulars talk too, and sometimes they say useful things.

Hacking

Encrypted files and protected terminals open a short hacking minigame. Win it to unlock the data.

Evidence

Everything Vance collects goes to the Evidence screen, with its full description: physical evidence, digital evidence and testimony. Reread the descriptions: the details (times, weights, names) are what link one clue to another.

Clues: connecting evidence

On the Clues screen, pick two pieces of evidence that explain each other. If the connection is right, Vance reaches a deduction. Deductions move the story to the next act, unlock confrontations and are the proof you'll need at the end.

Think in questions: How did the killer get in? When did the victim really die? Who locked the door? With what? Each answer usually brings two pieces of evidence together.

When you get stuck

In case 1, not every door opens with a badge. At a certain point, Vance will need help from people who live outside the law: someone in Chinatown knows a fixer, the street's holocall booths are the way to reach people who won't answer a traceable number, and favors are usually paid back with other favors. Keep an eye on the objective shown on screen.

In case 2, information has a price: some characters only talk after a favor, and certain places only appear on the Aerotrain once someone mentions them.

Danger and time

In some places Vance can walk into an ambush: find cover and react quickly. In the final act of case 2, part of the investigation is a race against the clock: once the countdown starts, don't waste time eating or wandering around.

Hints

Stuck? In case 1, talk to IRIS, the hotel's AI, and choose "★ I need a hint." In case 2, ask ÁGORA, Synapse's holographic assistant: "Any suggestions?". The objective at the top of the screen also always points to the next step.

6. The accusation

In the last act, the ⚖ Accuse button appears on the Clues screen. The game asks whether you want to gather everyone and, with the suspects present, you name the culprit and say whether anyone else should be charged.

  • The game has multiple endings. The outcome depends on whom you accuse and which deductions you have to prove it.
  • Naming the right culprit without enough proof is not enough.
  • There may be more than one person responsible. Accuse with care.
  • Each case has its own endings, from perfect to disastrous. In case 2, letting time run out also has consequences.
Before accusing, review your deductions. If there are still ⚠ confrontations pending or places you haven't visited, it may be too soon.

7. Life in the city

Hunger, Thirst and Fatigue

  • The three bars rise over time. Above 75%, a warning appears.
  • Near the limit, Vance slows down, his vision darkens and he dozes off on his feet.
  • If a bar reaches 100%, Vance passes out and wakes up hours later in his own bed.

Eating, drinking and resting

  • Food and drink machines at the hotel and the precinct.
  • Cafés, diners, convenience stores, pharmacies, bars and ramen shops around the city (see "Shops and backpack").
  • Chinatown stalls and restaurants: ramen, dim sum, tea, roast meats and a bakery.
  • At the apartment: fridge, stove (cooking), coffee maker and the bed (sleeping 6 hours resets fatigue).

Walking and driving

  • The city is open: explore on foot or in Vance's car, the Kestrel-7.
  • To drive, walk up to the car and press Drive. To park, slow down and press it again: the car pulls into the nearest curbside space by itself and Vance gets out. If you see "No curb space here", go a little further down the street and try again.
  • Chinatown is pedestrian-only. The Kestrel-7 stays in Sector 4: in the other districts, you get around on foot or by Aerotrain.
  • The GPS plots a route to your destination and shows directions at the top of the screen. The Call my car button brings the car to you.
  • The GPS starts with only a few addresses (the hotel, the café and the apartment). New places appear when someone mentions them. Each district has its own GPS.
  • Teleport (⚡): in the GPS, places you have already visited get a ⚡ button that takes Vance straight there. New places must be reached on foot, by car or by Aerotrain.

Aerotrain

  • The Aerotrain is an elevated maglev train that links the districts. Climb the station stairs, go past the ticket booth and the turnstiles and wait on the platform behind the glass doors. The train stops, the doors open and the panel shows the stops on your route.
  • Sector 4 and Sector 7 are always on the line, in both cases.
  • The Neo-Sampa stops (Paulista Sector, Bixiga, Minhocão and others) exist only in case 2. New stops join the route as the investigation progresses; an "AEROTRAIN: New stop" notice appears on screen.

Shops and backpack

  • Tap a shop's storefront (Enter) to go inside. At the counter, use Take items; the android clerk will chat too.
  • Everything is free (Vance is with the police). Limit of 5 units of each food, drink or medicine.
  • Types of shop: diner, pharmacy, café, convenience store, bar, ramen, clothing (Nyke, Havaionas), electronics (Pear, Samsong, Cyberdecks, Casas Bahya), implant clinic and pawnshop.
  • Backpack: 🎒 button or B key. Shows hunger, thirst and fatigue, your equipment and your items; tap an item to use it.
  • Equipment works while it's in the backpack: sneakers (+20% walking speed), jacket (fatigue 25% slower outdoors), flip-flops (10% slower), watch (needs shown as numbers), flashlight and night-vision implant (dark places brighter), metabolic implant (hunger and thirst rise 20% slower). The lighter and the disc have no effect.
  • The pawnshop only trades: offer an item from your backpack and choose another.

Settings

From the pause menu, ⚙ Settings: sound and music (on/off), music, effects and voice volume, language, zoom and fullscreen. Characters "speak" in a gibberish babble with their own pitch, Byte meows and the car has an engine roar.

Other things to do

  • Sit on benches, sofas, chairs and armchairs, and lie down on the bed.
  • Take a shower at home, watch the holo-TV and pet Byte.
  • At the Lotus Bathhouse: change in the locker room, wash up, get into the onsen and walk around in the water, brave the cold bath and the sauna.
  • In Chinatown: eat Mr. Wen's ramen and have Madame Lin read your cards.
  • Read the newsstand headlines, the holographic ads and the shop signs.

9. Places

Case 1 and Sector 4

Hotel Grand Alvorada: Presidential Suite 404

The crime scene. A decaying luxury suite at the top of the hotel, with purple carpet, a holographic table, floor-to-ceiling windows and acid rain streaming down outside. Forensics is still at work there. It's where the investigation begins: examine everything, with and without the scanner.

Hotel Grand Alvorada: 4th floor hallway

A long hallway with worn carpet, the porter's cleaning cart and a blinking food machine. The AI IRIS appears here to help.

Hotel Grand Alvorada: Lobby & Cyber Bar

The heart of the hotel: windows onto the neon city, velvet sofas, the front desk, a bar with a terminal and the elevator. The suspects hang around here. It opens up once Vance figures out something important about the suite.

District 4 Precinct

A large, dimly lit precinct with an android receptionist, officers' desks, Vance's case board, the captain's office, the forensics bench, an interrogation room and a break room with bad coffee.

Café Neon

A small, cozy café bathed in pink light, with an android barista and regulars who love a bit of gossip. A good place to pick up the city's rumors.

Vance's apartment

Living room, bedroom, kitchen and bathroom in a modest building. A place to sleep, cook, shower, watch the holo-TV and play with Byte.

Sector 4: the open city

A cyberpunk metropolis of 2085 under acid rain, explorable on foot or by car: wide avenues, parks, a plaza with a holographic fountain, a newsstand, holocall booths, shops, restaurants, and holographic ads and billboards atop the buildings, with parody brands like STARBYTES and RED VOLT.

Chinatown: Lotus Street

The oldest neighborhood in Sector 4, pedestrian-only. Red gates, lanterns, stalls, ramen, a fortune teller, teahouses (among them the discreet Matsurika Teahouse), an herbalist, dim sum, a bakery, the Mahjong Parlor and a koi pond. It appears on the GPS once someone mentions the neighborhood.

Lotus Bathhouse

A traditional Japanese sentō, open all night, with a front desk, locker room, washing area, mixed onsen, cold bath and sauna. Follow the house rules: bring a towel, and wash before getting into the water.

Other places

Some addresses only appear on the GPS after someone mentions them in conversation, such as the Matsurika Teahouse, the apartment of a certain hacker and a scrapyard in the south zone. Talk to everyone.

Sector 4 in case 2

The city from case 1 is still open in case 2, with the apartment, the café, the precinct and Chinatown. The Hotel Grand Alvorada, however, is sealed off with police tape and patrol cars at the door.

Sector 7: Industrial District (in both cases)

An open city like Sector 4, but made of foundries, warehouses, refineries and container yards, where the six o'clock siren sounds even in the dead of night. It is home to Vulcan Foundry, Warehouse 13, the Siren Bar and the Metalworkers' Union. You get there by Aerotrain.

Neo-Sampa (case 2)

Paulista Sector

The corporate district around the Neo-Sampa Tower, open like Sector 4: Av. Neo-Paulista with traffic and pedestrians, Trianon Park with real trees, trails and a lake, the MASP of the future (the glass block suspended from red porticos) and the elevated Aerotrain station, with stairs, ticket booth, turnstiles and glass platform doors.

Neo-Sampa Tower: Synapse Corp

Three hundred floors of glass. On the 220th are the Synapse atrium, with its holographic marble reception, and the shielded Kinetic Testing Lab, where Kaelen Voss was found. Other areas of the tower open up later in the case.

Bixiga

The bohemian neighborhood: old townhouses, cantinas, a wood-fired pizzeria, a used bookstore, a thrift shop, the Achiropita festival, the Vai-Vem samba school and the stairway. Here you'll find Zé do Tubo's Bar and Clube 14 Bis, which opens at midnight.

Baixo Minhocão: Sublevel 4

The city under the viaduct: shacks, fire barrels, stalls selling chips and prosthetics of dubious origin, a pastry stand and Tico's workshop. The upper city rushes by overhead without ever looking down.

10. Detective tips

  1. Examine everything twice: once with the scanner off and once with it on.
  2. Use the scanner on people, too.
  3. Reread the evidence descriptions. Times and numbers often match (or don't match) what other people say.
  4. When a ⚠ topic appears, go confront them. That's where the masks come off.
  5. No one is innocent just because they have a good alibi, or guilty just because they have a good motive.
  6. Leave the crime scene. The city holds parts of the story that nobody at the hotel or the tower will tell you.
  7. In case 2, once the clock starts ticking, go straight for the objective.
  8. Talk to everyone, including pedestrians and regulars.
  9. Keep Vance fed and rested: a passed-out detective solves nothing.
  10. When in doubt, ask IRIS (case 1) or ÁGORA (case 2) for a hint.
